FAQ

Good to know

Can I mix elements from different itineraries?

Absolutely: every itinerary is a starting point, not a fixed menu.

What's included / not included?

Hotels, activities, transfers, SIM card, and concierge support are covered per itinerary above. International flights, visa fees, and travel insurance are quoted separately; see below.

Do I need a visa for Oman?

Many nationalities, including French and UK citizens, can stay up to 14 days without paying for a visa, which is granted free on arrival or exempt depending on the passport. For longer stays you apply for a tourist visa online through the Royal Oman Police eVisa portal. We'll send you the exact steps once your trip is booked. Requirements and fees can change, so we always confirm the latest rules with you before you travel. See our Oman visa guide for details.

Do I need travel insurance?

Travel insurance isn't legally required to enter Oman, but we strongly recommend a policy covering medical care, evacuation, and trip cancellation, especially if your itinerary includes desert or mountain activities. We're happy to recommend providers or help you arrange coverage as part of your booking.
