About us

A different way to see Oman

Wadi Escape exists because Oman deserves better than generic tour packages.

We're a small, Oman-focused team built around one idea: the best trips come from real local knowledge, not a catalog of pre-packaged tours. Every itinerary is planned by people who know the country, from the wadis and dive sites to the mountain roads that don't show up on maps.

We work in two halves that meet in the middle: a team on the ground in Oman handling routes, guides, and logistics, and a planning team managing communication, bookings, and the details that make a trip feel effortless. No rush, no filler, no cookie-cutter itineraries.
