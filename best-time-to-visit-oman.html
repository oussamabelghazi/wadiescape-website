The best time to visit Oman

Which months suit which regions, and what to expect each season.

The short answer

For most of Oman, including Muscat, the coast, the interior and the desert, the best time to visit is the cooler season from about October to April. Daytime temperatures in winter are usually pleasant, which makes hiking, desert nights and sightseeing comfortable.

Winter (roughly November to March)

This is the high season for good reason: mild days, clear skies and little rain. It is the most comfortable time for wadi hikes such as Wadi Shab, for forts and souqs, and for a night in the Wahiba Sands. Book early, since availability tightens over this period.

Spring (March to April)

Warmer, but still comfortable for most plans. Spring is a good time for the mountains around Jabal Akhdar, where the terraced gardens are at their best, and for beaches and the sea.

Summer (roughly June to September)

Most of Oman is very hot and humid in summer, and it is not the best time for hiking or long days outdoors. Two exceptions are worth knowing: the mountains stay noticeably cooler than the coast, and the south around Salalah has the khareef, a monsoon season from late June to early September that turns the region green.

Autumn (October to early November)

Temperatures come down and the outdoors opens up again. October and November are a good window for hiking in the Hajar mountains and for the desert. Oman's National Day is on 18 November.

Choosing by activity

  • Wadis, hiking and desert camps: October to March
  • Muscat, forts and culture: all year, with winter the most comfortable
  • Beaches and the sea: autumn to spring
  • Salalah and the khareef: late June to early September

FAQ

Quick answers

What is the best month to visit Oman?

For most of the country the cooler months from October to April are best, with the middle of winter the most comfortable.

Is Oman too hot in summer?

Most of Oman is very hot and humid from June to September. The mountains are cooler, and Salalah has the khareef monsoon season from late June to early September.

What is the khareef?

The khareef is the monsoon season in Salalah and the Dhofar region, roughly late June to early September, when the area turns green and cooler.
