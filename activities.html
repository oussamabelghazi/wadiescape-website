Activities

What you'll experience

Every Wadi Escape trip is built from these experiences — mixed and matched to fit you.

Water & Coast

Dolphin Watching & Snorkeling Cruise — square photo (1:1)

Dolphin Watching & Snorkeling Cruise

Head out on the water to spot Oman's resident dolphin pods, then slip in for a snorkel over calm coastal reefs.

Duration: 3h Group size: 8–10 guests
Wakeboarding & Snorkeling Adventure — square photo (1:1)

Wakeboarding & Snorkeling Adventure

A more active day on the water — wakeboarding runs paired with a snorkel stop in a quieter cove.

Duration: 4h Group size: 4–6 guests
Kayaking & Stand-Up Paddle — square photo (1:1)

Kayaking & Stand-Up Paddle

A relaxed, scenic paddle along the coastline — suited to solo travelers, couples, or larger groups alike.

Group size: 10–80 guests
Al Khairan Beach Escape — square photo (1:1)

Al Khairan Beach Escape

A half-day escape to one of Oman's quieter beach lagoons — swimming, sun, and slow time by the water.

Duration: Half day

Mountains & Wadis

Muscat Mountain Hiking — square photo (1:1)

Muscat Mountain Hiking

Trails in the rocky hills right behind the capital — sweeping views over the city and the coast.

Group size: up to 15 guests
Wadi Hiking — square photo (1:1)

Wadi Hiking

Wadi Shab, Wadi Bani Khalid, Wadi Tiwi, and Snake Canyon — canyon rivers and pools carved through rock, at a range of intensities.

Snake Canyon: advanced level
Jabal Akhdar — square photo (1:1)

Jabal Akhdar

Oman's "Green Mountain" — terraced villages, rose gardens, and dramatic cliff-edge views, over an overnight stay.

Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night

Desert

Wahiba Sands — square photo (1:1)

Wahiba Sands

Rolling dunes as far as the eye can see. Visit for the day, or stay overnight at a luxury desert camp under a sky full of stars.

Day trip or overnight luxury camp
Barr Al Hikman camping — square photo (1:1)

Barr Al Hikman

A remote coastal desert peninsula, known for its flamingos and untouched flats — camping far from anything else.

Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night

Culture

Nizwa Heritage Tour — square photo (1:1)

Nizwa Heritage Tour

Nizwa Fort and its souq, Jabreen Castle, Bahla Fort, and a stop at an Omani halwa factory — a full day through Oman's historic interior.

Sur Coastal Tour — square photo (1:1)

Sur Coastal Tour

The historic dhow-building city of Sur, with a seasonal visit to the Ras Al Jinz turtle reserve where you can watch sea turtles nest.

Ras Al Jinz visit: seasonal
Muscat city experiences — square photo (1:1)

Muscat City Experiences

The Grand Mosque, Muttrah Corniche and Souq, Old Muscat, and Al Alam Palace — the capital's landmarks in one day.
