How many days do you need in Oman?

What fits in 4, 6, 8 or 11 days, and how to choose.

Oman is bigger than it looks on a map, and its best places are spread out: the capital and coast, the mountains and forts of the interior, and the desert. The right length depends on how much of that you want to see without rushing between them.

4 days: Muscat and the coast nearby

Four days is enough for a first taste of Oman's capital: the Grand Mosque, Muttrah Corniche and Souq, Old Muscat, a dolphin watching and snorkeling cruise, and a free day. This is the format of our Muscat Escape.

6 days: add the coast and the wadis

With six days you can leave Muscat and drive the coastal road: Wadi Shab, Bimmah Sinkhole, Sur and its dhow shipyard, plus a beach stop at Al Khairan. See our Oman Coast Explorer and our Wadi Shab guide.

8 days: mountains, forts and a desert night

Eight days opens up the interior: Nizwa Fort and Souq, Jabreen Castle, Bahla Fort, two days in Jabal Akhdar, and a night at a desert camp in the Wahiba Sands. This is the route of our Desert & Mountains Adventure.

11 days: the complete journey

Eleven days lets you combine the coast, the mountains and the desert at a relaxed pace, with extra time for a night of camping at Barr Al Hikman and a fuller cultural programme. That is our Ultimate Oman Experience.

How to choose

  • First time in Oman and short on time: 4 days in Muscat, or 6 days if you want the coast too
  • You want desert and mountains: 8 days is the practical minimum
  • You want to see the whole country without rushing: 10 days or more
  • Travelling in the hot months: the season changes what is comfortable, see when to visit Oman

Every itinerary can be adjusted, and any duration is possible with a custom trip.
